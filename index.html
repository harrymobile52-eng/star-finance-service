Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services

What are the eligibility criteria for a Personal Loan?

To qualify for a Personal Loan, you must be aged 21-60 years, have a minimum monthly income as per lender eligibility criteria, and possess a clean credit score of 650 or above. Salaried employees and self-employed professionals are both eligible.

What documents are required for a Home Loan?

Home Loan applications require identity proof, address proof, income documents (salary slips or IT returns), bank statements for the last 6 months, and property documents. Additional documents may be requested based on your profile.

How can I apply for a Credit Card?

You can apply online through our website, visit any branch, or call our customer service. The application requires Basic KYC documents, income proof, and a credit score report. Approval usually takes 3-5 business days.

What types of Insurance do you offer?

We offer comprehensive insurance solutions including Life Insurance (term, whole life, ULIP), Health Insurance (individual, family, senior citizen), and Motor Insurance (comprehensive, third-party, zero-depreciation).

How is loan eligibility determined?

Loan eligibility is calculated based on your monthly income, existing EMIs, credit score, employment type, and debt-to-income ratio. Our Eligibility Checker tool provides instant results without affecting your credit score.

How long does it take for loan approval?

Loan approval time varies by product: Personal Loans (24-48 hours), Home Loans (7-14 business days), Business Loans (3-5 business days). Pre-approved customers receive instant disbursal. Documentation completeness affects processing time.
